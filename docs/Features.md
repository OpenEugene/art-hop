# Features

Past Art Hop features. Each month we spotlight a venue, an artist, or a show.

- [Wheelhaus Arts](Features/Wheelhaus-Arts.md), July 4th, 2026. A youth arts space run by local painter and muralist David Placencia celebrates its grand opening in the Warehouse District.
- [Viking Brewing](Features/Viking-Brewing.md), May 2nd, 2026. One of our founding venues, with Romanelli Revival, Bootleg Rose, and the art of Cristal Waterhouse and Shanna Trumbly.
- [Easter The Show](Features/Easter-The-Show.md), April 4th, 2026. Cowdog Productions' semi-secular Easter variety show at Caffe Pacori.
- [Pinebox Night Market](Features/Pinebox-Night-Market.md), March 7th, 2026. Pinebox Collective's micro-market, a blacksmithing demo, and Emerald City Roadkill.
- [Dead Rockstars](Features/Dead-Rockstars.md), February 7th, 2026. A pop art tribute show with over 40 artists at Blunt Graffix.
