# Westish

Westish, on South Bertelsen Road, hosts The Painted Porch during the Art Hop: live music, column painting, and conversations on the porch. [Learn more at PaintedPorch.fyi.](http://PaintedPorch.fyi)

**Open Eugene Holiday Party:** Westish hosts the Open Eugene Holiday Party on Saturday, December 5, noon to 5pm, during the December Art Hop and Winter Wonderfest. Stop by and celebrate with us.

1009 S Bertelsen Rd, Eugene, OR 97402
