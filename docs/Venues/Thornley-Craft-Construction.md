# Thornley Craft Construction

Thornley Craft Construction LLC is a Bailey Hill Road workshop hosting multiple resident artists: Josh Thornley (mixed media and wooden art from up-cycled materials), John of Alejandro Leather (handmade leather goods, alejandroleather.com), and Eep (illuminated objects made with metal, fire, and electricity). 1123 Bailey Hill Rd, Units 30 & 31.
