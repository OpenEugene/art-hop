# Studio West

Studio West is an art glass studio and gallery opening its doors during The Art Hop to welcome visitors to see live glass blowing demos and finished works. Check them out during the Hop, sign up for a class or take home one of their finished pieces! [View Studio West's website here.](https://visitstudiowest.com/)
