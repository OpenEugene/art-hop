# Blunt Graffix

While you're touring the western-most end of the Warehouse District, be sure to swing in to Blunt Graffix, a creative studio specializing in printmaking. Blunt Graffix is marked by its pop-art style renderings of subjects. Their prints often weave bold color with holographic reveal layers amid captivating detail and cinematic perspectives; all of which somehow manages to solicit some feeling of bad-assery within the viewer. [View Blunt Graffix website here.](https://bluntgraffix.com/)
