# Fitti's Pizza

Fitti's Pizza is family owned and operated, serving up Detroit-style pizza with a northwest twist. Visit them during The Art Hop for a deep dish bite of 'za and check out the various local artists and musicians they are supporting by offering their shop as a show space.
