# Wheelhaus Arts

Wheelhaus Arts is an incredible arts space dedicated to cultivating the arts for artists of all ages. Run by artist David Placencia, Wheelhaus Arts hosts workshops, community events, gallery shows and more, all aiming to expand the arts in our community! Stop by to see the latest from this vibrant community of artists. [View Wheelhaus Arts website here.](https://wheelhausarts.square.site/)
