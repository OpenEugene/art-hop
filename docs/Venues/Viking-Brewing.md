# Viking Brewing

A charming micro brewery sits nested in West Eugene's industrial Warehouse District boasting home brewed beer, top notch pub fare, live music and rotating monthly visual artists. Whether you're visiting the peak of summer picnicking under the hop vines, or cozied up inside the pub in the winter, Viking Brewing is a great place to eat, drink and be merry. [View the Viking Brewing website here.](https://www.drinkviking.com/)
