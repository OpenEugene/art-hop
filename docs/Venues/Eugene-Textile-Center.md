# Eugene Textile Center

The Eugene Textile Center is an enormous and dynamic fiber artist's heaven. ETC is a large fiber arts store, classroom and gallery in one. They sell both new and second hand, and estate fibers, yarns, looms and more. Be sure to pop in and check out their gallery, their workshops and more. [View Eugene Textile Center's website here.](https://www.eugenetextilecenter.com/)
