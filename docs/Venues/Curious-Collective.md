# Curious Collective

Curious Collective is probably the newest artist collective to grace the Warehouse District. We are stoked to have them on board with us and can't wait to see what they get up to. Check out resident artists' woodworking, photography, costuming, digital art, jewelry, games, furniture, and larger construction-related projects. [View resident Chalan Moon's Etsy shop here.](https://www.etsy.com/shop/mooncreationsllc/)
