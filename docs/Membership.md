# Membership

Support the Art Hop! Become a contributing member.

Become a monthly member of The Art Hop not only to help us cover the bases, but to help us grow into a stronger organization that aims to promote the economic vitality of artists in the Warehouse District community.

Your contribution to The Art Hop ensures our independence as an organization and allows us to stay focused on our artists, on your experience, and in our neighborhood.

Currently all of our membership options will lend support to The Art Hop to cover operating costs. We are currently 100% self-funded. Your contribution covers hours spent on coordinating, map making, marketing, poster printing, website development, event planning, and more. We can't wait to grow our membership pool to be able to hire a photographer and a part-time marketing staff member. Currently we are simply focused on creating a stable position for our organizer. In the meantime, we will keep innovating and creating the best event we can!

Stay tuned here for updates on our budget, goals, and membership. We look forward to offering member benefits in the future, but for now we are targeting sustainability for our organization.
