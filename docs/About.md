# About

## By community, for community

The Art Hop had its very first event in August of 2025. It was initiated by local artist, jeweler, and multitool-human Sarah Bush of [FRIPP jewelry](https://www.fripp.design/), alongside the robust partnership of several Warehouse District venues, including Spetzler Designs, Jud and Renee Turner at the Oblivion Gallery, Jonna Threlkeld at Viking Brewing, Eric Pierce and Dena Zehava at Caffe Pacori, and Matt Dye of Blunt Graffix. The group met for the first time on May 12, 2025.

> "It has been wonderful to be one of the founding members of the Warehouse District Art Hop. To be part of something intentional. Meeting so many wonderful artists and making new friends in the community. I have regular visitors to my shop that I can feel becoming lifelong friends and supporters of this west Eugene event. Thank you for being the spark and heart of so much connection."
>
> An Art Hop founding venue
